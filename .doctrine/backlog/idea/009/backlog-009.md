# Bridge autonomy modes

SL-009 DEC-070 bounds a bridge pairing at 10 bridge-started SATAN turns per
operator refill. Operator (2026-10-29): "I expect to come back to this ..
might want to allow full autonomy until compaction, or shorter drives. If
it's cheap to reconnect it matters less."

Candidates: a per-pairing budget chosen at pair; "until compaction" as the
bound; a drive length set by the operator per refill. DEC-070 keeps the
policy as one function over the pairing document so this is a local change.
