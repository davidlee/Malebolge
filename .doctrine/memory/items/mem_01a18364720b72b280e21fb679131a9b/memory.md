Observed in SL-006's design run (doctrine design materialise, 2026-10-29):
sections are written to design.md in lexical order of their ids, so sec-10
comes before sec-2. The run has no ordering field and no verb to rename or
retire a section.

How to apply: when a design will have ten or more sections, declare ids whose
text order is the reading order (e.g. reserve sec-1..sec-9 and order the rest
by text), or keep content under ids already placed where it should read.
SL-006 reassigned bodies between ids to fix the order; cite its sections by
title, not id.

Separately: in drafting and reviewing, the selector runbook step
(draft.selectors, review.selectors) completes only with an explicit
`discharge` declaration in `doctrine design apply`, after the selectors are
recorded.
